# DIRECTION: BONTRIVO · Quandoo-Alternative (film 2)

The anti-sameness check runs against **two** references here: the skill's reference film
and film 1 („Serviert auf dem O“), which is the last film made with this skill.

## Derivation

1. **Brand:** as in film 1. Tomato, cream, ink, olive as the rare signal, Montserrat,
   and the O as a ring.
2. **Category expectation:** „Every Quandoo alternative is advertising with the
   countdown right now: dates, a red clock, ‚Jetzt wechseln!‘“ (foratable, teburio,
   gastrobird and others publish on the same subject). Decision: **break it.** No
   countdown, no fear clock.
3. **Context:** feed, muted, short attention span. The date has to read in second 1, the
   claim at the turn.
4. **Emotional shape:** loss → independence. That is not relief (film 1) but **„power or
   trust“**: something that belongs to you. Feeling: a broken path is closed again, and
   this time it belongs to you.

## Three directions

### A · „Argument, dann Beweis“ (inherited from the reference film, **killed**)
Problem → agitation → thesis → UI beats → proof → contrast → CTA, dark/light, cursor.
Automatically one of the three under the skill's rule, automatically killed. On top of
that there is no real product UI.

### B · „Der Countdown“ (**killed**)
```
Thesis     A split-flap board (railway departures) flips through the Quandoo dates; on 31.12.
           it flips to „DIREKT BEI IHNEN“
Dials      high energy · ink · flat · fixed · mono figures · board texture ·
           red/white · type only · ticking SFX · narrated
Signature  the flip board
Risk       fear marketing; the fact turns into panic
Wrong for  a brand that wants to sound warm and helpful
```
Killed by question 3: **any competitor can run the same countdown unchanged, and they do
right now.** Also question 1: a countdown sells urgency, not independence.

### C · „Der Weg gehört Ihnen“ (**chosen**)
```
Thesis     A single tomato line IS the reservation: from the guest to the restaurant.
           In the portal world it runs through someone else, and breaks. At the turn it runs
           straight to you, and at the end it closes into the O of the logo.
Dials      Energy    tight and urgent in the first half (the break), calm and steady after the turn
           Density   one line plus few labels; diagram, not illustration
           Ground    ink (portal world) → cream (own channel); flipped at the turn by a wipe
                     along the line
           Depth     strictly flat, NO shadows (film 1 had overhead depth through shadows)
           Camera    a lateral journey along the line: the frames push into each other, so the
                     line runs through the whole film (film 1: fixed overhead)
           Type      Montserrat; the dates as huge tabular figures, labels mixed case 700
                     (film 1: everything uppercase 900)
           Texture   clinical, no grain, no grade (film 1: grain)
           Colour    ink/cream, tomato ONLY as the line and the price, olive only for „verbunden“
           Product   diagram of the channels (Website-Widget, Google-Profil, Instagram) +
                     the real price; no UI mock-up
           Sound     a held tone that FOLLOWS the line and STOPS at the break (silence as the
                     cue); after the turn it comes back brighter and resolves in the brand chord
           Voice     narrated + dates/claim as type
Signature  see below
Risk       a diagram can look sober and businesslike; the line has to have weight and speed
Wrong for  a product without a „path“ (anything whose value is not a connection)
```

**Assessment.** (1) Serves the claim: „direkt bei Ihnen“ IS a straight line. (2) Brand:
the line closes into the brand's O. (3) A competitor? The ending, the line becoming the
Bontrivo O, cannot be borrowed. (4) Survives a bad day: one line, a few labels.

## The signature

> **The reservation is one line. It breaks with the portal, and it closes into the O of
> BONTRIVO.**

- **Early (F01):** the line runs from the guest via „Quandoo“ and breaks on 1 October.
  The tone breaks off.
- **Middle (F05/F06):** the turn: the line runs straight into the O. Then the channels
  connect to it (built: Speisekarte and Google-Profil, the two the voice names).
- **Resolution (F09):** the line wraps around a circle and thickens into the ring of the
  logo. The bean lands: connected.

## Structure against the two references

| Axis | Reference film | Film 1 | **Film 2** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Device | argument → proof | menu in courses | **one path: broken → owned** |
| Arc | problem → solution | cold open on the answer | **problem first, deliberately long** |
| Turn | 18 % | 0 % | **~45 %** |
| Opening | empty ground | claim as type | **mid-motion: a line already running** |
| Climax | element fills the frame | pull-back: plate = O | **a line closes into a ring** |
| Ending | quiet lockup | loud, logo assembles | **the ring is drawn, CTA + disclaimer, the tone resolves** |
| Sound dramaturgy | sparse → held tone | a recurring clink | **held tone that breaks off (silence = problem)** |
| Camera | turn + dolly | fixed + 2 moves | **lateral journey (push chain along the line)** |
| Depth | 3D turn | shadows | **flat, none** |

More than four dials and every structural axis differ from film 1.

## Register map and accent budget

- **Ink:** F01–F04 (Quandoo, the break, the dead ends, „the next portal“).
- **Cream:** F05–F09 (own channel). The flip at F05 is the story turn: a wipe follows the
  line.
- **Tomato:** (1) the line, (2) the price, (3) the CTA button in F09 (added during the
  build: it is the site's own button colour and the only other solid tomato shape). It
  does not mark any headline word, because the line is the emphasis.
- **Olive:** (1) the moment the line connects (F05), (2) the bean in the ring (F09).

## Anti-sameness check

- Inherited from a worked direction or from film 1? A killed; film 1's plate/serve
  vocabulary is deliberately not used (no plate, no shadow, no clink scale).
- Kinetic type on dark as the first shot? Partly: the date on ink. But it opens
  **mid-motion with the line**, not with type that builds up.
- Cursor? None.
- Could the closest competitor use this unchanged? No: the line ends in the Bontrivo O.
