# BRIEF: BONTRIVO · Quandoo-Alternative (film 2)

## The product

- **What it is:** the same product as in film 1 (restaurant website with reservation,
  pre-ordering, menu and hosting at a fixed monthly price), positioned as the switch for
  restaurants whose online reservations ran through Quandoo.
- **What it replaces:** the Quandoo widget, the booking link in the Google profile, the
  portal profile, and with them the dependence on a portal.

## The audience

- **Who is watching:** restaurant owners who used Quandoo until 30 September 2026, and
  since 1 October have no working online reservation channel.
- **What they are about to do:** „einfach das nächste Portal suchen“ (site copy).
- **What they are sceptical about:** effort just before the Christmas season, costs,
  the next dependency.
- **Where it plays:** Instagram/Facebook (muted → claim and dates as text, captions,
  9:16), the `/quandoo-alternative` landing page, sales conversations.

## The ONE claim

> **Reservierungen ab jetzt direkt bei Ihnen.**

The CTA headline of all three pages, verbatim, five words. The before/after in one
sentence: portal → you.

- **Why it is true:** own website with reservation (product), fixed price with no
  commission (pricing card), real help with the switch (support from Hamburg).
- **What we do NOT claim:** no savings figure, no „beste Alternative“, nothing about the
  data transfer from Quandoo to Bontrivo (the FAQ asks about it, the answer was not
  supplied), nothing about existing Quandoo bookings (sources nuance it differently).

## Real assets

As in film 1: logo (vectorised from the supplied PNG), brand tokens, Montserrat (OFL),
the site's icons. **No Quandoo assets:** no logo, no Quandoo colours, no Quandoo UI. The
name appears only as plain text in the film's own typeface.

## Negative list (additions to film 1)

- No Quandoo logo, no Quandoo colours, no imitated Quandoo widget.
- No mockery, no „gescheitert“, no gloating: the shutdown is stated as a fact, by date.
- No countdown figure (it would be false from the next day on).
- No claim about data migration or existing bookings.
- **Deliberate exception to the skill rule „No competitor named on screen“:** the
  shutdown of a named platform is the subject. The film names it factually, like the
  source page, and ends with Bontrivo's disclaimer. **Before publication: have the rules on
  comparative advertising (UWG §6) checked.** This note does not replace legal advice.
