# BRIEF: BONTRIVO · Vorbestellen & Abholen (film 3)

## The product

- **What it is:** the pre-order function of the Bontrivo restaurant website („Pick Up Order
  System“): guests order ahead and pick up; the team sees incoming orders in real time and
  can control the pickup time. Included in the base price.
- **What it replaces:** the phone call at peak time, the guest waiting at the counter, or a
  delivery/ordering portal between guest and restaurant („ohne Umweg über Drittportale“).

## The audience

- **Who is watching:** owners of restaurants, bistros, cafés with takeaway; Bontrivo
  prospects and existing customers who have not switched pre-ordering on.
- **What they are about to do:** keep taking orders by phone, or not offer pickup at all.
- **What they are sceptical about:** one more thing for the team to watch during service;
  guests arriving at the wrong time.
- **Where it plays:** Instagram/Facebook (muted → captions, 9:16), YouTube, the pickup
  solution page, sales conversations.

## The ONE claim

> **Einfach für Gäste. Einfach für Ihr Team.**

Verbatim from the site (menu teaser of the pickup page). It names both sides, which is the
film's device.

- **Why it is true (what is shown):** guests order ahead on the restaurant's own website;
  the team sees the order in real time and sets the pickup time; the guest picks up on time.
- **What we do NOT claim:** no ordering UI, no device for the team, no online payment, no
  figures about waiting time or satisfaction, no delivery (pickup only).
