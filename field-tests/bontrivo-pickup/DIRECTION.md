# DIRECTION: BONTRIVO · Vorbestellen & Abholen (film 3)

The anti-sameness check runs against **three** references: the skill's reference film,
film 1 („Serviert auf dem O“) and film 2 („Der Weg gehört Ihnen“).

## Derivation

1. **Brand:** as before: tomato, cream, ink, olive as the rare signal, Montserrat.
2. **Category expectation:** pickup / takeaway ordering is advertised with a phone in a
   hand, a cart, a „Bestellung eingegangen!“ notification, a scooter. Decision: **break
   it.** No phone, no UI (none is supplied anyway), no delivery.
3. **Context:** feed, muted, and the site's own claim names two parties: „einfach für
   Gäste, einfach für Ihr Team“. A muted viewer must see both sides at once.
4. **Emotional shape:** reassurance, two-sided: the guest gets their food on time, the team
   stays in control. Not relief (film 1), not independence (film 2): **„everyone knows
   where they stand“**.

## Three directions

### A · „Argument, dann Beweis“ (inherited from the reference film, **killed**)
Automatically one of the three, automatically killed. And there is no real product UI to
prove anything with.

### B · „Die Uhr im O“ (**killed**)
```
Thesis     The logo's O is a clock face, the bean its hand; the pickup time is the notch.
           When the bean lands in the notch, the order is ready, on time.
Signature  the bean travelling round the O into its notch
Risk       a clock film tips into countdown and urgency
```
Killed by **law 10 (no house style)**: it would be the third film in a row whose device is
the logo's O turning into something (plate, ring, clock). The brand gets a recognisable
lockup; it does not need a recurring trick. (Also dropped early: „Die Schlange“, a queue
that disappears. It needs countable people, which reads as a waiting-time figure that is
not supplied, and any pickup tool could run it unchanged.)

### C · „Zwei Seiten, eine Zeit“ (**chosen**)
```
Thesis     The frame is split for the whole film: the guest's side (cream, left) and the
           team's side (ink, right). The seam in the middle is the counter. The order
           appears on both sides at the same instant (real time), the team sets the pickup
           time on one clock that straddles the counter, and the bag crosses the counter
           exactly at that time (pünktlich).
Dials      Energy    calm, precise; every event is a small, exact click
           Density   two icons, one clock, short type hugging the seam
           Ground    cream AND ink at the same time, side by side, all film long
                     (films 1 and 2 switched register in time; this one splits it in space)
           Depth     flat
           Camera    locked, symmetric stage; no transitions, only hard cuts on the constant
                     split (film 1: fixed + 2 moves; film 2: push chain)
           Type      mirror-set: guest copy right-aligned to the seam, team copy left-aligned
                     to it, so every pair of lines reads ACROSS the counter
           Texture   none
           Colour    tomato = the order and the pickup time (and the price, the CTA);
                     olive = „fertig/abholbereit“ (the check on the bag) and the bean
           Product   the act, not the interface: guest, order, team, clock, bag
           Sound     STEREO as the device: the guest's sounds sit left, the team's right; the
                     real-time moment sounds on both sides at once; the bag crosses the
                     stereo field with the counter; a service bell at the pass on „pünktlich“
           Voice     narrated, the copy split across the seam
Signature  see below
Risk       a symmetric split can look static; every beat has to happen ON the seam
Wrong for  a product with one party (anything without a counter between two people)
```

**Assessment.** (1) Serves the claim: „einfach für Gäste, einfach für Ihr Team“ IS the
split. (2) Brand: cream and ink, the two registers of all three films, side by side; the
lockup straddles them. (3) A competitor? The split could be borrowed, but the claim on it
is Bontrivo's own sentence and the ending is the Bontrivo mark across both sides.
(4) Survives a bad day: one stage, few objects, the copy carries without motion.

## The signature

> **Two sides, one time: the bag crosses the counter on the minute the team set.**

- **Early (F03):** the order appears on both sides in the same frame (and in both ears).
- **Middle (F04/F05):** the team sets the pickup time on the clock that straddles the
  counter; the hand reaches it and the bag crosses on „pünktlich“; the bell rings once.
- **Resolution (F07, F10):** the claim set across the counter (left: „Einfach für Gäste.“,
  right: „Einfach für Ihr Team.“); the lockup sits across the seam in two colourways.

## Structure against the three references

| Axis | Reference film | Film 1 | Film 2 | **Film 3** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Device | argument → proof | menu in courses | one path: broken → owned | **two sides, one time (split screen, parallel action)** |
| Arc | problem → solution | cold open on the answer | problem first, turn at 43 % | **no problem act: setup of both sides → convergence on the minute → claim** |
| Opening | empty ground | claim as type | mid-motion line | **the frame splits: the team's side opens from the seam** |
| Climax | element fills the frame | pull-back: plate = O | line closes into the ring | **the bag crosses the counter on the minute** |
| Ending | quiet lockup | logo assembles | ring drawn into the logo | **lockup across the seam, two colourways, no transformation** |
| Register | dark → light | ink ↔ cream in time | ink → cream once | **ink and cream at once, all film** |
| Sound | sparse → held tone | a recurring clink | a tone that breaks off | **stereo split, service bell** |
| Camera | turn + dolly | fixed + 2 moves | push chain | **locked symmetric stage, hard cuts only** |
| Depth | 3D turn | shadows | flat | flat |

Every structural axis differs from both earlier films; depth repeats film 2 (flat).

## Register map and accent budget

- **Cream:** the guest's side (left; top in 9:16). **Ink:** the team's side (right; bottom).
- **Tomato:** (1) the order tag, (2) the pickup-time arc on the clock, (3) the price,
  (4) the CTA button.
- **Olive:** (1) the check on the bag when it is handed over, (2) the bean in the logo.

## Anti-sameness check

- Inherited from a worked direction or an earlier film? A killed; B killed for being
  film 1/2's trick a third time; no plate, no serve, no line-into-the-O.
- Kinetic type on dark as the first shot? No: the first shot is the split itself.
- Cursor? None. Phone? None.
- Could the closest competitor use this unchanged? The stage, perhaps; the claim on it and
  the ending, no.
